ICT killzone times (New York time)

ICT Silver Bullet times

Three one-hour windows (New York time): 3:00–4:00 AM (London), 10:00–11:00 AM (NY AM) and 2:00–3:00 PM (NY PM).

ICT macro times

Short windows where price often seeks liquidity or rebalances (New York time): 2:33–3:00, 4:03–4:30, 8:50–9:10, 9:50–10:10, 10:50–11:10, 11:50–12:10, 1:10–1:40 PM and 3:15–3:45 PM.

Why a news lock?

High-impact releases such as CPI, NFP and FOMC can spike spreads and slippage within seconds, and many prop firms ban trading around them. This tool marks every high-impact release for the currencies you trade and turns the status red for a few minutes on each side (adjustable).

FAQ

What time is the London killzone in UK time?

2:00–5:00 AM New York is normally 7:00–10:00 AM in London. During the few weeks when US and UK daylight saving are out of sync it shifts by an hour. The clock above always shows the correct local time.

What time is the New York killzone in GMT / UTC?

7:00–10:00 AM New York is 11:00–14:00 UTC in US summer (EDT) and 12:00–15:00 UTC in US winter (EST).

Do killzones apply to gold, indices and crypto?

Yes. Killzones are time-based, so traders apply them to XAUUSD, NQ/ES and major crypto pairs as well as forex. Indices trade mainly in the NY AM and NY PM windows.

Does it work on weekends?

Forex closes Friday 5:00 PM New York time and reopens Sunday 5:00 PM. The status shows CLOSED with a countdown to the open.
